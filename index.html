The practice

This isn’t a programme. It’s a practice.

Three things. That’s it.

Every Monday

The Monday Prompt

One question in your inbox. Thirty seconds to read. Designed to stay with you all week — surfacing in quiet moments, interrupting assumptions. You don’t have to respond. Just let it work.

Once a month, live

The Monthly Master Class

60 to 75 minutes with Elly or Chris on one aspect of regenerative leadership. A real learning experience and an introduction to dialogue. Not a webinar, not a pitch and not recorded — the live room is where it happens.

Small and growing

The Practice Library

Six to eight short, applicable practices at launch — each a single page. No courses, no gated content. Each one can be used in a team meeting this week. The library grows slowly, by design.

Plainly

A quick word about what you won’t find here.

There are no badges, no leaderboards, no activity scores. No pressure to post, no expectation of visible engagement.

There is no course map, no completion certificate, no curriculum to get through. The Threshold is not a structured programme, and we’d rather say so now than waste your time. If that is what you are after, the Circle is shaped differently — start there instead.

What there is: one question a week worth sitting with. One substantial conversation a month worth showing up for. A small library of practices worth trying. That is enough. For now, it is everything.

The hosts

About Elly and Chris

Chris Jackson and Dr Elena Hinova-McNamee are the co-founders of Teams of Tomorrow. Together they curate spaces which inspire and enable founders, CEOs and leadership teams to navigate the increasing complexity of modern leadership. Their work combines leadership transformation, team coaching, peer learning and thriving leadership practices to enable leaders to build confidence, belonging and clarity.

We ask questions rather than hand over answers. Given the right conditions — time, space, other leaders in the room — most leaders discover they already know more than they think.

When it fits

When you’re ready to go further

The Threshold is yours to use at whatever depth suits you. There is no expectation of you to go further, and there is no pressure from us to do so.

Twice a year, a small cohort of leaders gather in the Circle — eight people, twelve weeks, doing the real work together. Threshold members hear about each intake one week before applications open publicly.

If that feels like a path you’d like to take, you’ll know when the time is right. You’ll have spent enough Monday mornings with the questions to know whether the answers matter enough to go deeper.
